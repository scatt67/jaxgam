    Phase 2: the operator fit¶

    Scope¶

    This page covers FitControl(execution="operator", linear_solver="pcg"): matrix-free fitting for models whose random effects have too many levels for a dense or discretized design. It describes how preparation bounds the model, the matrix-free actions, the frozen system and its positive-definiteness certificate, preconditioned conjugate gradients (PCG), the fixed-smoothing coefficient driver, the operator Fellner-Schall (EFS) controller with its score error bounds, score assembly, and the bulk link-scale variance. Routing into the route is in the fit lifecycle; the dense EFS it is compared with is in the dense fit.

The The modules of jaxgam/formula/operator/ import no JAX.

    A planned refactor will move this route onto a shared engine; see the unified fit design.

    How it works¶

    Entry and refusals¶

    GAM._fit_operator refuses, before any source access: linear_solver other than "pcg"; result other than "prediction"; uncertainty other than "none"; sp=None unless optimizer="efs"; an estimated theta (use NegativeBinomial(theta, fixed=True)) or more than one theta; a device other than None or "cpu". The data must be a RowSource that owns weights and offset. With sp=None and optimizer="efs" it calls _fit_operator_efs; otherwise it validates sp (length, real, positive, finite; a quick length check before preparation when every smooth is a plain s() without by, and the exact check against penalties.n_penalties after it), prepares the model, and calls fit_operator_fixed_sp. A formula with no penalty still needs sp=[].

    Prepared geometry¶

    prepare_operator_model(spec, source, control=OperatorPreparationControl(...)) splits the model into two parts and never builds an n-by-p matrix, a dense random-effect design or a p-by-p penalty:

    • Indexed random effects (s(..., bs="re")): an IndexedRandomEffect holds the term's factors (FactorEncoding, a frozen level tuple and lookup) and numeric variables. evaluate(columns) returns one level id and one amplitude per row: the id is the mixed-radix product of the factor codes, numeric variables multiply the amplitude (random slopes), and an unknown level or a non-finite amplitude contributes zero. The width is the product of the level counts. The penalty is one IdentityPenalty(width, scale) per term, with scale the squared largest absolute amplitude seen in training. Levels are a categorical's declared categories (unused ones included), otherwise the sorted observed values; a missing training level is an error.
    • Modest block: intercept, numeric parametric terms and s/te/ti smooths with cr, cs, cc, tp, ts, gp bases (with numeric or factor by). Its raw width is capped by OperatorControl.max_modest_coefficients (default 64) before anything is built. Plain cubic terms go through the ordinary prepare_model; any by, tensor or radial term goes through prepare_modest_cubic_by, which calls prepare_radial_smooth for tp/ts/gp (knots harvested from bounded scans, mgcv's sampler run locally). A modest penalty block is copied into the global PenaltyStructure at its global columns.

    Every scan runs through _CheckedSource, which checks positions, validity, finite response, weights and offset, the column schema, and pickles and SHA-256-hashes every row to prove identical replay (source_replay_digest), charging each row's bound before serializing it. Memory is checked by _check_budget at each stage; failures are MemoryError before allocation. The result PreparedOperatorModel records metadata_bound_bytes, preparation_peak_bound_bytes and preparation scan counts, and evaluate_batch turns a RowBatch into an OperatorBatch (modest rows plus (ids, amplitudes, start, size) per indexed term) under batch_workspace_bound.

    Matrix-free actions¶

    to_device_operator_batch validates one OperatorBatch (finite modest values, ids in range, non-overlapping coefficient intervals), charges its transfer, and makes a DeviceOperatorBatch with DeviceIndexedRows. The kernels in jaxgam/fitting/operator/actions.py follow Xbd/XWyd's gather/reduce contract in public coordinates:

    • matvec_batch: modest_values @ v[modest_columns] plus, per indexed term, amplitudes * v[start + ids].
    • rmatvec_batch: scatter-add of modest_values.T @ u and of amplitudes * u into start + ids.
    • normal_matvec_batch and accumulate_normal_matvec_batch: X'W X v, the latter adding into a donated p-vector across host batches so no extra p-vector is allocated per batch; accumulate_rmatvec_batch likewise for right-hand sides and gradients.
    • penalty_matvec: S_lambda v from local penalty blocks (penalty_ops.apply, mgcv's Sl.Sb pattern).

    operator_family_rows (jaxgam/fitting/operator/family.py) evaluates one batch's family rows at eta: it takes the first and second derivatives of the row deviance with JAX AD, giving half_gradient = D'/2, the observed curvature observed_weight = D''/2, and the working response W (eta - offset) - D'/2 (the form pinned gam.fit4 uses, which avoids dividing by zero curvature). There is no Fisher substitution or curvature clipping; it requires the family capabilities row_separable, observed_information, direct_deviance and differentiable_deviance.

    The frozen system and its certificate¶

    FrozenScanSystem(model, source, log_sp, curvature_from_rows=..., memory_budget_bytes=..., use_coarse=...) represents H = X'WX + S_lambda at one frozen curvature. Construction:

    1. Checks the construction bound (65p + 288K^2 + 32pK and further terms, where K is the modest width) and copies and freezes the modest metadata and penalties.
    2. Weights the penalties with the C library exp. Each modest penalty must be positive semidefinite (ValueError otherwise); every other block must be a full-rank IdentityPenalty (NotImplementedError otherwise, though preparation never builds one).
    3. One certificate scan accumulates the modest Gram matrix (also split into positive- and negative-curvature parts), the assembled diagonal, indexed off-diagonal bounds, the indexed-to-modest coupling (p by K), and a SHA-256 digest of the row curvature.
    4. Certifies positive definiteness without forming H:
    5. With no negative rows: S_modest + G / (1 + t) must have a Cholesky factor, where t is the trace of the indexed Gram relative to the indexed penalty (an upper bound on its spectral radius).
    6. With negative rows and no indexed effect: S_modest + G itself must have a Cholesky factor.
    7. With negative rows and indexed effects: first a Schur-complement test that replaces the indexed block by a lower bound on its diagonal (diagonal minus the off-diagonal bound), when that bound is positive; otherwise a leverage test (total negative-row leverage below one in a block lower-bound metric); otherwise a low-rank certificate for at most _MAX_SIGNED_DOWNDATE_RANK = 8 independent negative directions; otherwise LinAlgError("Actual signed system has no sufficient Schur SPD certificate.").
    8. The data curvature must be positive definite on the modest penalty's null directions (LinAlgError otherwise). The certificate is sufficient, not necessary: a refusal does not prove H is indefinite (tests/test_execution/test_operator.py, test_signed_certificate_cap_is_not_a_claim_of_indefiniteness).
    9. Builds the preconditioner (below), compiles the normal-action and penalty kernels, reads their measured scratch, and checks the action peak bound.

    matvec(v) is one complete checked source scan: start from S_lambda v, add each batch's X'WXv, and verify that the curvature digest is unchanged. Each matvec, right-hand side and objective is therefore one full scan.

    has_exact_diagonal (one indexed effect and nothing else) and has_exact_arrowhead (one modest block of at most 64 columns coupled to one indexed identity block) mark the structured shapes for which the log-determinant and traces are computed exactly.

    Preconditioned conjugate gradients¶

    prepare_preconditioner builds an InversePreconditioner: the reciprocal of the assembled diagonal for every coordinate, a Cholesky-factored SmallBlockInverse for the modest columns (G + S_modest), and with OperatorControl.use_coarse=True an additive CoarseInverse on the modest penalty-null directions. Large blocks retain only the diagonal; the construction bound is 17p plus the capped block terms.

    solve_host_pcg (reached through FrozenScanSystem.solve) charges 14 float64 p-vectors plus the measured scratch of its four compiled kernels before allocating, then iterates on the host with JIT steps from jaxgam/fitting/operator/iterative.py. The threshold is max(atol, rtol * ||rhs||). A recursive residual below the threshold only moves the state to VERIFY_RESIDUAL; convergence (CONVERGED) needs a fresh true residual rhs - H x from another matvec, and a failed verification restarts CG without resetting the iteration count. Other statuses are MAX_ITERATIONS, NONFINITE and NONPOSITIVE_CURVATURE. Every matvec is counted in HostPCGResult.matvec_count.

    The fixed-smoothing coefficient driver¶

    fit_operator_fixed_sp is a host observed-Newton loop:

    1. Budget. fit_bound (metadata, family snapshot, 80p for retained vectors, extra terms for no-intercept layouts, 128 * max_iterations) plus a row bound plus the measured scratch of the compiled family, forward and adjoint kernels must fit memory_budget_bytes; the remainder is the frozen system's budget.
    2. Start. _initial_null_beta ports mgcv's get.null.coef for layouts it can prove: with an intercept, the intercept alone at link(mean(initialized y)); for no-intercept layouts, bounded source QR projections, some of which refuse with NotImplementedError. Unknown-scale families also reduce a null scale, used in the convergence test and reported as initial_null_scale.
    3. Each iteration builds a FrozenOperatorWorkingState (iteration one at the family's mustart, later at the current beta), a FrozenScanSystem with its observed curvature, the right-hand side X'(W(eta - offset) - D'/2) by _reduce_rhs, and solves H beta_new = rhs by PCG. A failed PCG ends the loop with converged=False and the last accepted state.
    4. Gaussian identity (single_step_pirls) is solved once and refined against its true residual up to _STRICTLY_ADDITIVE_REFINEMENTS = 3 times, then accepted.
    5. Step halving: while the penalized deviance _reduce_objective (direct row deviance plus beta'S beta, infinite outside the domain) exceeds the previous value plus a small allowance, halve toward the previous beta; after 100 halvings RuntimeError.
    6. Convergence: relative objective change below OperatorControl.tolerance and 2 max|X'D'/2 + S beta| <= tolerance * (|objective| + null scale). At max_iterations the result is returned with converged=False.

    Every arrow into the source is a complete, replay-checked scan, so the scan count per Newton iteration is roughly one certificate, one right-hand side, one per PCG matvec, at least one objective and one gradient.

    OperatorFitResult carries coefficients, log_lambda, the model, the family snapshot, deviance, converged, n_iter, convergence_info and OperatorFitDiagnostics: source counters, initialization_scans, rhs_scans, objective_scans, certificate_scans, certificate_actions, krylov_actions, krylov_iterations, completed_solves (Newton solves only), peak_bound_bytes, the three measured kernel temporaries, accepted_objectives, inner_status (the last PCG status), final_gradient_norm, initial_null_scale, the preparation_* counts, efs_indeterminate_comparisons, and preconditioner sizes reported apart from the peak. There is no PCG residual norm in the fit diagnostics.

    GAMPredictionResult._from_operator_fit keeps an OperatorPredictionGeometry in the predictor, score=None, edf_total=None, no covariance, scale=1.0 for known-scale families and None otherwise, lambda_strategy="fixed", execution_route="operator", and fit-only source_scans and batches_scanned.

    The arrowhead exact algebra¶

    For H = [[A, B'], [B, D]] with a positive diagonal indexed block D, ExactArrowheadFactorization.from_system replays one checked scan to recover the coupling B (FrozenScanSystem.exact_arrowhead_coupling, which also checks the curvature digest), forms the modest Schur complement A - B' D^-1 B and factors it. It gives log_determinant, inverse_diagonal, modest_inverse, and spectral lower and condition estimates in O(pK + K^2) storage. Its floating result is conditional on the assembled H; it is not by itself a score error bound.

    The operator Fellner-Schall controller¶

    _fit_operator_efs admits only:

    • Poisson with the log link;
    • batch_rows from 64 to 65536 (checked before any scan);
    • after preparation, exactly one indexed effect with 257 <= L <= 4096 levels and L <= n_obs <= 16 L (CALIBRATED_OPERATOR_EFS_DOMAIN.admits), plus at most CALIBRATED_OPERATOR_EFS_MAX_MODEST = 4 modest columns, and no other coefficients;
    • from the start provider, a factor-only effect with an identity penalty and unit weights and zero offset (NotImplementedError otherwise).

    It splits memory_budget_bytes: one eighth for preparation and the start, a quarter for each refit, half for the score components, and the rest less a result reserve as the aggregate budget. prepare_indexed_operator_efs_start replays the initial.spg working diagonal and balances one smoothing parameter by factors of 10. SourceOperatorEFSFitProvider refits with fit_operator_fixed_sp and evaluates states with MatchedOperatorEFSProvider; run_operator_efs_known_scale runs _run_operator_efs, which follows pinned efsudr (mgcv R/gam.fit4.r) for known scale.

    Matched components (MatchedOperatorEFSProvider.evaluate, all at one beta, theta and rho, bound by operator_efs_fit_fingerprint):

    • score rows: MatchedOperatorScoreReducer.reduce, one replay giving penalized deviance, the saturated log-likelihood term and diagnostics such as unit_weights, zero_offset, maximum_response, maximum_row_l1_norm;
    • log|S|+ and its gradient: LocalOperatorPenaltyDeterminant, with the range fixed by the unscaled local penalties;
    • log det H on an observed-curvature system: exact_structured_diagonal (decimal logarithms with a rounding bound), exact_structured_arrowhead, or otherwise the stochastic estimate (Rademacher probes and Lanczos);
    • Fisher traces tr(H_F^-1 S_j) and quadratics beta'S_j beta on a second system with Fisher curvature (FrozenOperatorWorkingState.fisher_curvature): structured for the diagonal and arrowhead shapes, else coordinate solves capped at 256 coordinates (ExactOperatorPenaltyStatistics);
    • EDF p - sum_j lambda_j tr(H_F^-1 S_j).

    assemble_operator_score combines them with operator_reml_score: pdev / (2 phi) - ls + (log|H| - log|S|+) / 2 - M_p log(2 pi phi) / 2, where M_p is the penalty null dimension. The observed log-determinant is used, not the Fisher one. For every shape GAM.fit admits, the determinant and traces are structured, so no probes or Lanczos run; the OperatorLogDetControl(probes=4, lanczos_depth=4) passed by _fit_operator_efs only sizes the output reservation.

    Score bounds. admitted_operator_efs_score_bound returns a bound and a method label, "indexed_poisson_empirical_v2", "arrowhead_poisson_empirical_v2" (or "indexed_gaussian_empirical_v2", whose Gaussian runner run_operator_efs_gaussian_scale GAM.fit cannot reach), or (None, "unavailable"). Each bound is the total of an OperatorScoreEnvelope:

    • arithmetic: 32 * eps * (n + p) * M (plus K^2 for the arrowhead), where M sums the score's term magnitudes;
    • coefficient: the effect of the final coefficient residual, from the final gradient and the identity-penalty floor (indexed) or the Schur spectral lower estimate (arrowhead);
    • determinant: the structured log-determinant's rounding bound.

    A state is admitted only inside the calibrated domain and with: unit weights, zero offset, Poisson responses at most 32, converged PCG, tolerance <= 1e-7, cg_rtol <= 1e-10, cg_atol <= 1e-12, final gradient at most 1e-5, all |beta| <= 20, and conditioning gates (diagonal condition ratio at most 1e4 and minimum diagonal at least 1e-6 for the indexed shape; spectral lower estimate at least 1e-3 and condition at most 1e5 for the arrowhead). The three control thresholds equal the OperatorControl defaults, so loosening any one leaves every comparison undecided. The code calls these empirical envelopes, not certificates.

    Comparison. compare_operator_scores requires identical probes and strategy. With both bounds present and radius r = bound_old + bound_trial, the trial is an improvement if diff + r < 0, worsening if diff - r > 0, equal only if both diff and r are zero, and otherwise indeterminate; a missing bound is always indeterminate. The paired standard error is reported but never decides. Only a stochastic_lanczos score is refined (probes and depth doubled up to 256 and 128); structured scores return indeterminate directly.

    _raw_ratio is efsudr's max(0, exp(-rho) d - t) / max(0, q) * phi (1 when both numerator and q are zero, 1e6 when non-finite), and _trial_rho clips at EFSControl.log_lambda_max. The first fit is at the start plus 2.5 on the log scale; if it does not converge the controller raises RuntimeError. An undecided extension is declined and counted. The score window (_score_window_certified) needs the last four accepted scores and bounds, with every adjacent change plus both bounds below EFSControl.score_tolerance. score_window and deviance_change are converged outcomes, except that convergence on the last allowed outer iteration is relabelled iteration_limit; indeterminate_score, iteration_limit, inner_failure and invalid_update are not converged. No exception or warning is raised for an indeterminate stop: the result has converged=False, convergence_info set to the stop reason and operator_diagnostics.efs_indeterminate_comparisons.

    The EFS result adds score, score_error_bound, score_bound_method, edf_total, scale and score_scale (1.0), lambda_strategy="efs_reml", n_iter as outer iterations, cumulative source_scans, and known_workspace_bytes. Its operator_diagnostics describe only the accepted state's refit.

    Calibration. The domain and envelope constants were measured with scripts/benchmark_operator_efs_calibration.py (make benchmark-operator-efs-calibration) against an independent 50-digit decimal REML reference (tests/operator_efs_calibration.py). The tests in tests/test_execution/test_operator_efs_calibrated.py check that at domain boundaries the matched-state score error stays inside the admitted bound, that just outside the domain (or past a conditioning gate) the bound is refused, and that the domain constants are the recorded ones; complete operator EFS fits are compared with dense EFS. tests/test_validation_matrix.py compares operator EFS for y~x+s(g,bs='re') with pinned R at STRICT, using tightened EFS and operator controls.

    Uncertainty¶

    Operator fits retain no covariance: predict(se_fit=True) raises and materialize_covariance has no provider. The uncertainty path is GAMPredictionResult.bulk_link_variance(training_source, calibration_data, *, probes, seed, memory_budget_bytes, control=None, scale=None, max_calibration_standard_errors=5.0), which returns a (BulkOperatorVariance, BulkVarianceCalibration) pair:

    1. It requires a converged operator result, unchanged family and theta, and the original training source (row count and fingerprint). Known-scale families use their scale; others need scale=.
    2. It re-runs prepare_operator_model on the training source (the replay digest must match), builds a FrozenScanSystem with Fisher curvature at the fitted coefficients and smoothing parameters, using half the budget, and PCGControl() defaults (rtol=1e-10, atol=0, 1000 iterations) unless control is passed. FitControl.operator is not consulted.
    3. BulkOperatorVariance.build draws probes (at least 2) right-hand sides b = X'W^(1/2) z + S^(1/2) q (one scan each, local penalty blocks factored with null spaces preserved by _penalty_noise) and solves H u = b for each, keeping a probes-by-p sketch. Since E[(x'u)^2] = x'H^-1 x, the variance of a row is scale * mean((x'u)^2) with a Monte Carlo standard error. A failed probe solve raises BulkVarianceSolveError; no partial sketch is kept.
    4. calibrate(calibration_data) solves exactly for at most min(256, batch_rows) selected rows with SelectedOperatorVariance (one PCG solve per row), and maps each row's pivot probes * estimate / exact, which is chi-square with probes degrees of freedom, to an equivalent standard normal deviate (equivalent_normal_deviates). bulk_link_variance raises RuntimeError when the largest absolute deviate exceeds max_calibration_standard_errors.
    5. estimate_iter(source) then streams any prediction source in batches, yielding BulkVarianceBatch(positions, variance, monte_carlo_se) with a link_se property. Results are on the link scale only.

    The calibration checks the selected rows only; it does not bound unselected rows or the iterative-solve bias, which BulkVarianceDiagnostics reports separately as maximum_relative_residual. tests/test_execution/test_operator_bulk_variance.py compares the exact selected-row variances with pinned R's squared link-scale standard errors at STRICT and checks that a biased sketch fails calibration.

    Memory bounds¶

    Every stage checks a prospective bound before allocating and raises MemoryError: preparation through _check_budget and per-row charges; fit_operator_fixed_sp through fit_bound plus measured kernel scratch; FrozenScanSystem through its construction and action peak bounds; solve_host_pcg through 14 p-vectors plus measured scratch; prepare_preconditioner through 17p plus block terms; the EFS provider through reserve on its aggregate budget. No p-by-p array is formed for the indexed coefficients; the indexed width is not capped by max_modest_coefficients. The reported byte figures (peak_bound_bytes, known_workspace_bytes) are accounting bounds that exclude source storage and native allocator or compiler memory.

    Rules and invariants¶

    • No dense design, penalty or covariance. The route never forms X, a p-by-p penalty or a covariance; tests/test_execution/test_operator_fit.py fails a fit that calls PenaltyStructure.materialize, and tests/test_scaling_allocation_guards.py checks that the dense design builder never sees the training rows.
    • Replay identity. Every scan is checked against the preparation replay digest and every system action against its curvature digest (tests/test_execution/test_operator.py, test_selected_repeated_lineage_and_frozen_curvature_replay).
    • Certified SPD before PCG. solve_host_pcg assumes the caller certified the system; sampled CG curvature does not certify it. Certificate failures raise from GAM.fit rather than returning converged=False.
    • Convergence needs a true residual (tests/test_fitting/test_iterative.py, test_recursive_residual_cannot_claim_convergence_and_false_candidate_restarts).
    • Observed curvature, never Fisher, in the fit; Fisher curvature only in the EFS traces and the bulk variance, each on its own frozen system.
    • Budgets before allocation (test_memory_preflight_rejects_before_source_scan_predictor_or_transfer, test_fit_combined_budget_rejects_before_initial_source_pass, tests/test_fitting/test_preconditioners.py).
    • R parity. Fixed-sp coefficients, deviance and fitted values are compared with pinned R for Gaussian, Poisson, Binomial, Gamma and fixed-theta NB links in tests/test_execution/test_operator_fit.py (test_operator_observed_fixed_sp_fit_matches_pinned_family_reference), and through GAM.fit in tests/test_validation_matrix.py.
    • EFS decisions only with admitted bounds (tests/test_execution/test_operator_score.py, test_common_probe_difference_reports_noise_but_waits_for_calibration).
    • Phase 1 stays NumPy. The modules of jaxgam/formula/operator/ import no JAX.

    Pitfalls¶

    • fit() defaults to result="full"; this route needs result="prediction".
    • family="nb" is an estimated theta and is refused; use NegativeBinomial(theta, fixed=True).
    • Loosening OperatorControl.tolerance, cg_rtol or cg_atol from their defaults removes every EFS bound, so an EFS fit ends indeterminate_score after one outer iteration with no warning. A Poisson response above 32 does the same.
    • Non-unit weights, an offset, a penalized modest smooth or a random slope are hard NotImplementedError refusals under operator EFS (from the start provider), not indeterminate outcomes.
    • Parametric terms must be numeric; a factor main effect is refused in preparation.
    • A gp penalty can keep signed eigenvalues; a modest penalty with a negative eigenvalue is refused in FrozenScanSystem.
    • No-intercept layouts depend on source null projections, several of which refuse with NotImplementedError.
    • Scan counts are high: every matvec, right-hand side, objective and gradient re-reads and re-hashes every row.
    • The fit and the result use different batch sizes on a small source. The prepared model carries the smaller of FitControl.batch_rows and the source's row count (_operator_fit_batch_rows in jaxgam/api.py), because the kernels pad every batch to that size and every bound charges it. OperatorPredictionGeometry keeps FitControl.batch_rows, so a prediction request may be larger than the training data.
    • PCGControl is not exported from the top-level package; import it from jaxgam/execution/operator/system.py.

    Where to change things¶

    • Admit a new basis in the modest block: prepare_operator_model's term check and jaxgam/formula/operator/modest.py (or jaxgam/formula/operator/radial.py), plus its evaluator bound in jaxgam/formula/operator/workspace.py.
    • Change the actions: jaxgam/fitting/operator/actions.py; keep to_device_operator_batch's validation and charges in step.
    • Change the SPD certificate or preconditioner: FrozenScanSystem.__init__ in jaxgam/execution/operator/system.py and jaxgam/fitting/operator/preconditioners.py.
    • Change PCG: solve_host_pcg and jaxgam/fitting/operator/iterative.py; keep the true-residual rule.
    • Change the Newton driver, start or step halving: fit_operator_fixed_sp and _initial_null_beta in jaxgam/execution/operator/fit.py.
    • Widen operator EFS admission: the domain, envelopes and admitted_operator_efs_score_bound in jaxgam/execution/operator/efs_controller.py, _fit_operator_efs in jaxgam/api.py, and the start provider; re-measure with scripts/benchmark_operator_efs_calibration.py and update tests/test_execution/test_operator_efs_calibrated.py.
    • Change score assembly: jaxgam/execution/operator/score.py and jaxgam/fitting/operator/efs_score.py.
    • Change bulk variance: jaxgam/execution/operator/bulk_variance.py and GAMPredictionResult.bulk_link_variance in jaxgam/results.py.
